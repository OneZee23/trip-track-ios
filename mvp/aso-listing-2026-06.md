Your road diary, automatically. Records every drive — route, photos, stops. Stays on your iPhone. Optional cloud sync. Free, open source.

*168 chars.*

### Description (4000 chars max)

```
Your personal road diary — recorded automatically.

TripTrack records every drive you take: route, speed, stops, photos. Open the app later and see what your year actually looked like on the road. No manual logging, no setup per trip.

Most trip apps are built for the gap year — once-a-year vacations across five countries. TripTrack is built for the trips you take every weekend without thinking. The drive to the cabin. The route to your parents. The favorite ride on Sunday morning. They add up to something — and now you can see it.

— RECORD —
• Automatic trip detection — starts when you start driving
• Background GPS with Kalman filter for accurate routes
• Speed, altitude, and distance in real time
• Add photos along the route
• Apple Watch companion (Live Activity support)

— RELIVE —
• Trip feed grouped by month, contribution calendar
• Smooth animated route playback
• Speed-colored route on the map
• Altitude profile and stats per trip
• Auto-generated trip titles from geocoding

— EXPLORE —
• Territory tracking (fog-of-war map showing where you've been)
• Total stats across your driving history
• Filter by region or vehicle

— YOUR DATA, YOUR CONTROL —
• Private by default — every trip starts on your device
• Optional cloud sync with Apple Sign-In if you want a second device
• Optional social feed — share specific trips, not everything
• No ads. Optional crash reports & anonymous usage stats — both 1-tap off
• Open source — code is on GitHub, including the backend

Free to use. No subscription required. Optional cloud sync may become a paid option in the future to cover server costs.

iPhone, iOS 17+. Native Swift, no external dependencies.

Made by a solo developer over 18 months. Feedback welcome.
```
*~1700 chars — leaves room for translation expansion in DE.*

## Submit checklist для App Store Connect

В **первый день** challenge (1 июня):

- [ ] Заменить DE title/subtitle/description/keywords (полностью переписать сломанные)
- [ ] Заменить EN title/subtitle/description/keywords
- [ ] Обновить promotional text для EN + DE
- [ ] Проверить screenshot localization status (overlay-текст в DE-store должен быть DE — если нет, добавить в W2 task)
- [ ] Submit for review (не в выходные — review faster на буднях)
- [ ] Tag в App Store Connect: "Privacy" категория настройки проверить
- [ ] Privacy Labels — verify актуальны для 0.5.5 (sign in with Apple, optional cloud sync)
- [ ] App Review Notes (`docs/app-review-notes.md`) — обновить если есть новые потенциальные questions от Apple
